# Topical Authority Map — Concrete.co.id

Status: lean planning artifact  
Evidence snapshot: homepage and 123 URLs in `page-sitemap.xml`, reviewed 2026-07-25 (Asia/Jakarta)  
Catalog target: 10 parent topics and 60 distinct article briefs

Concrete.co.id is the broad decision and learning hub for concrete materials, specification, supply, execution, quality, durability, and ownership. Existing commercial and taxonomy routes remain the destination for product or service intent. The planned `/artikel/[slug].html` pages answer narrower informational decisions and must not imitate those landing pages.

`precast.concrete.co.id` remains a separate specialist property. Cross-domain overlap is allowed, but Concrete.co.id articles should explain selection and interfaces rather than reproduce a complete specialist precast catalog.

## Portfolio and route boundaries

- Existing root routes own broad commercial or taxonomy intent, including `/ready-mix`, `/beton-precast`, `/decorative`, `/mesin`, `/jasa`, `/jenis`, and their children.
- Planned articles answer one decision each and link to existing routes when that route is the useful next action.
- Grade labels such as K-250 are not treated as complete specifications. Project requirements, applicable standards, exposure, execution, and acceptance evidence still govern.
- Concrete.co.id is not a substitute for a licensed engineer, competent safety professional, testing laboratory, approved project documents, or the full text of applicable standards.
- Exact prices, capacities, mix proportions, test limits, tolerances, legal duties, product availability, and performance claims require current project-specific evidence.

## Internal-linking shape

Each article may link to:

1. the most relevant existing commercial or taxonomy route;
2. one prerequisite article that helps the reader understand the decision;
3. one execution, testing, durability, or procurement consequence;
4. a specialist sibling property only when it is genuinely the better next resource.

Links must help the reader progress. Repeating a keyword-rich anchor or inserting every available link is not useful internal linking.
